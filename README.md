# The-Nine-World
The 9 are a group of 9 original characters I created. Each has their own name, appearance, personality, backstory and abilities. Some are heroes, others are villains, and some are mysterious. They include Shadow Death, Niren, Shadowy, Omey, Darkshadow, Darkmater, Omega, EXE and Puppeteer. All 9 are part of my imagination and creativity.
